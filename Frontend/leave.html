Sick Leave (SL)

  • Initial balance: 8 days per year
  • Requires supporting document for leaves longer than 3 days
  • Can be availed during probation period

Privilege Leave (PL)

  • Accrual rate: 1.5 days per month
  • Available only after probation period (6 months)
  • Automatic update on 5th of each month
  • Joining date rules apply:
    • Joined on or before 15th: Count from joining month
    • Joined after 15th: Count from next month
Probation Period

During probation (first 6 months), PL balance shows as 0. Once status changes to active, PL is calculated as 1.5 × completed probation months.
